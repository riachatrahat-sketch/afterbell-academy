Afterbell Academy theme v5

Install: WordPress admin > Appearance > Themes > Add New > Upload Theme > choose afterbell-academy.zip > Activate.
Then Settings > Permalinks > Save once.

On activation the theme creates: sample tracks, subjects and 14 sample classes, plus these pages:
Homeschool, 1:1 Tutoring, Explore categories, Become a teacher, Educator library, Schools and partners,
Gift cards, About, Blog, My account, Terms, Privacy, Children's privacy, and a main menu.
It also turns on "Anyone can register" so "Join for free" works (Settings > General to change).

Replace the sample classes ("Sample teacher") and the starter legal text before launch.
Customize: Appearance > Customize > Afterbell homepage (headline, text, currency, contact email).

Forms (tutor request, teacher application, partner, gift card) send email to the contact email.
Transcripts, Transactions and Messages in My account are placeholders until checkout is connected.

v5 additions
- Pages: Log in, Sign up, Welcome (5-step onboarding), Settings, Membership, Join membership, Help center, Refer and earn.
- Teachers: new Teachers menu in admin. Pick a teacher on each class; profiles appear at /teacher/name.
- Membership plans and prices are SAMPLE values: edit under Appearance > Customize > Afterbell homepage.
- Online payment is not connected. The checkout sends a membership request by email.
- Social login (Google, Facebook, Apple) appears automatically if you install the free "Nextend Social Login" plugin.
- Teacher trust line (for example about vetting) is empty by default. Only add statements that are true.
